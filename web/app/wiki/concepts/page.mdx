# How it works

Provenance does not tag individual items. It keeps accounts, like a bank: for every place that holds items and every
type of item there, it knows **how many** there are and **whose** they are, as shares.

## Holders

A holder is anything that holds items:

- **A player.** Inventory, ender chest, the item on the cursor and the crafting grid count as one holder.
- **A container block.** Chests, barrels, hoppers, furnaces, shelves, pots, lecterns, jukeboxes and so on. A double
  chest is one holder.
- **An entity.** An item lying on the ground, a minecart with a chest, a donkey, an item frame, an armor stand, a mob
  that picked something up.
- **A shulker box or bundle**, together with what is inside it. Moving the box moves its contents.

Moving items inside one holder (between slots of one chest) changes nothing.

## Pools and mixing

Each holder has one pool per item type. Items in a pool are interchangeable, so their shares mix.

> Alice puts 10 diamonds she mined into a chest. Bob adds 10 of his own. The chest now holds 20 diamonds,
> **Alice 50%, Bob 50%**. Whoever takes 5 out gets 5 diamonds that are half Alice's, half Bob's.

This is why answers are shares and not "these exact 3 diamonds": in Minecraft, two diamonds in one chest are the same
thing.

Items with their own components get their own pool: an enchanted or renamed sword is
`minecraft:diamond_sword#3f9a…`, not a plain `minecraft:diamond_sword`. Wear and the contents of shulker boxes and
bundles do not split pools, so a pickaxe does not turn into a new item with every block it breaks.

## Origin

**Origin** says who produced the items:

| Origin | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| a player name | Mined, killed, fished or otherwise produced by that player |
| `mob` | Dropped by a mob nobody killed |
| `loot` | Found in a loot chest |
| `trade` | Bought from a villager or a wandering trader |
| `world` | Dropped by the world on its own: washed-away flowers, decayed leaves, explosions |
| `preexisting` | Already there when Provenance first looked: items older than the plugin |
| `creative` | Taken in creative mode |
| `unknown` | Appeared without any event that explains them |
| `other` | The sum of many small shares (only the 16 largest are kept by name) |

Origin survives processing. Crafting a diamond block from Alice's diamonds gives a block that is Alice's; smelting,
brewing, cooking on a campfire, enchanting, the anvil, the grindstone and the smithing table work the same way.
Otherwise crafting would wash anything clean.

## Custody

**Custody** says which player last put the items into a holder. In a player's own inventory, custody is that player.

Custody is what makes theft visible: when Bob takes diamonds from a chest Alice filled, the record says Bob received
items **held by Alice**, whoever originally mined them.

## Reconcile: the safety net

Not everything in the game has an event Provenance can follow: placing blocks, eating, tools breaking, other plugins
giving or taking items. So from time to time Provenance compares the real contents of a holder with its record. This
happens when a player joins and leaves, on the first click in a container, before dropping and picking up, and every
`reconcile-interval-seconds` (5 by default).

- **Fewer items than recorded** — the missing ones are written off.
- **More items than recorded** — the extra ones are recorded as `unknown` (or `creative` for a player in creative).

So `/give`, a dupe glitch or a plugin that hands out items all end up as `unknown`, sooner or later. A precise event
only adds who did it.

## Links between players

From all of this Provenance computes, per player:

- **Sent / received** — how many items went from one player to another, directly or through chests, drops and
  hoppers.
- **Wealth by origin** — whose items a player holds, including what they keep in chests.
- **Similarity** — how alike two players' wealth is. Two accounts fed from the same source look alike even if they
  never traded directly.

See [`/prov player`](/wiki/commands#prov-player-name) and [`/prov links`](/wiki/commands#prov-links-name).

## History

Every change is also written to a journal: what moved, from where to where, why (the cause), and when. The journal is
what `/prov history` and the [web viewer](/wiki/web-viewer) read. Causes you will see:

`mine`, `kill`, `loot`, `pickup`, `drop`, `container`, `craft`, `hopper`, `smelt`, `trade`, `consume`, `place`,
`break`, `shoot`, `fuel`, `despawn`, `destroy`, `reconcile`, `sync`.

To keep the journal small, back-to-back hopper moves are merged, and events of one kind in the same chunk and hour are
added up into one line: a mob farm is a few lines per hour, not thousands. Events are kept for 30 days, pure
automation (block to block) for 3. All of this is set in the [configuration](/wiki/configuration#journal).
