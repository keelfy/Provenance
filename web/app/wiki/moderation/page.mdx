# Moderation recipes

## Someone is selling too many diamonds

1. `/prov player <name>` — what share of their diamonds is `unknown`? A few percent is normal noise; a large share is
   not.
2. `/prov history player <name> diamond` — look for **red** lines: diamonds that appeared from nowhere, and when.
3. Check the cause of the red line. `reconcile` means Provenance found the items during a routine check, without any
   event that explains them: a dupe, `/give`, or a plugin that hands out items.
4. `/prov web history player <name> diamond` — open the graph and see where the items went next: who got a share of
   them.

> `preexisting` is not suspicious: those items were there before Provenance was installed. `creative` is red too,
> because creative items in survival hands usually should not be there.

## Items went missing from a chest

1. Look at the chest and run `/prov history block`.
2. Lines like `world 120 64 -33 -> Bob 5 diamond (was held by Alice)` show who took items and whose they were.
3. `/prov web history block` shows where the thief took them after, `web.depth` holders deep.

## A player says they got everything legitimately

`/prov player <name>` breaks their wealth down by origin. Mostly their own name, `mob`, `loot`, `trade` is a player
who earns their items. Mostly one other player's name is items that came from that player, through trades, chests or
drops.

## Alt accounts and trading rings

`/prov links <name>`:

- High **received** from one player and little sent back is a feeder.
- High **similarity** with no direct trade means the two accounts are fed from the same source.

## A farm floods the history

Mob farms and hoppers are summed up by chunk and hour, and pure automation is kept for only 3 days. If the noise is
still in the way, use the item filter (`/prov history player <name> <item>`), or in the web viewer turn on **Group
similar** and **Fold drops**.
