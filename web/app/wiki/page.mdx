# Provenance

Provenance is a Paper plugin that remembers where items came from. It follows every item as it is mined, looted,
crafted, put into chests, dropped and picked up, and can tell you at any moment **whose items a player is
carrying** and **how they got them**.

It is built for moderators. The questions it answers:

- **Dupes.** Items that appeared from nowhere are recorded as `unknown` and shown in red in the history.
- **Theft.** When a player takes items from a chest, the history says whose items they were.
- **Laundering.** Passing items through a friend or a chest does not wash them: the share of the original owner
  travels with them, through crafting and smelting too.
- **Alt accounts and trading rings.** Who sent what to whom, and which players hold suspiciously similar wealth.

Nothing is written into the items themselves. Stacks stay stackable, and players see no difference.

| Server | Paper 26.3 |
| Java | 25 (what Paper 26 needs) |
| Storage | SQLite out of the box; MySQL / MariaDB or PostgreSQL optional |
